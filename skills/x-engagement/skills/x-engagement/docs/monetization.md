# Monetization: Original Content Rewards

X's creator payout program. It replaces **Creator Revenue Sharing**, which X retired
because its incentives were misaligned — it paid for engagement volume rather than for
bringing net new content to the platform.

This matters to the playbook because **the payout currency and the distribution currency
are now the same thing**: verified impressions on original posts in the Home Timeline.
Optimizing for the algorithm and optimizing for revenue are no longer separate exercises.

## How Earnings Are Calculated

Eligible creators earn on **qualified impressions** — impressions generated when
**Premium (verified) users** view their **original posts** on the **Home Timeline**.

Three filters stack, and each one is strict:

| Filter | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Premium viewers only | Impressions from non-Premium accounts don't pay |
| Home Timeline only | Profile visits, search, notifications, external embeds don't pay |
| Original posts only | Replies don't pay; reposts and thin aggregation are devalued |

Payouts run **every two weeks**, with a **$30 minimum** before a payout is released.

## Eligibility

| Requirement | Threshold |
|-------------|-----------|
| Age | 18+ |
| Location | Country where the program is available |
| Account type | Personal or Business — political/government orgs excluded |
| Subscription | Active X Premium |
| Verified followers | ≥ 500 |
| Verified impressions | ≥ 500,000 in the trailing 90 days |
| Standing | No repeated Monetization Guidelines / ToS violations |
| Track record | Consistent history of publishing original content |

500,000 verified impressions / 90 days ≈ **5,500 verified impressions per day**, sustained.
That is not reachable through replies or through follower-only reach — it requires
repeatedly clearing the Banger Screen into out-of-network distribution.
→ See **[Content Quality Gate](content-quality.md)**.

## What Counts as Original

Any format, as long as the work is yours: posts, threads, Articles, video, images, memes.
X names original reporting, analysis, commentary, expertise, and creativity.

**Qualifies:**
- First-hand reporting, investigation, or lived experience
- Self-recorded video and native-to-X media
- Analysis or commentary that meaningfully transforms its source material
- Research threads that add new understanding

**Devalued or excluded:**
- Reposting headlines/clips with minimal addition
- Low-effort aggregation, "BREAKING"-style repackaging
- Thin attribution — borrowing an idea without transforming it
- Volume strategies that optimize post count over substance
- Reply farming (reply impressions do not count toward monetization)

You are responsible for holding the rights, permissions, or licenses for anything you
didn't create. IP-infringing content is not eligible and is not permitted on X.

## Enforcement Direction

Alongside the program, X has been tightening the same lever on the old system:

- Aggregator payouts cut to 60% of prior levels, with a further ~20% reduction signalled
- Permanent deductions floated for habitual bait posters (repeated "BREAKING" packaging)
- Reply impressions removed from monetization to curb engagement farming
- Tooling under development to identify the **original author** of a piece of content and
  route revenue to them

X's stated position: reposts and commentary remain a core pillar of the platform, they just
shouldn't be paid the same as original creation.

## Strategic Implications for the Playbook

1. **Out-of-network reach is now revenue.** The Banger Screen (`quality_score ≥ 0.4`) was
   previously a distribution gate; it is now also a paywall. Content that only reaches your
   existing followers earns almost nothing at the required scale.
2. **Replies are a distribution tool, not an income stream.** This reinforces the existing
   rule: post to your own profile first, then reply into threads to pull traffic back.
   Never spend your best material inside someone else's thread — it can't be monetized there.
3. **Premium-follower composition matters more than raw follower count.** Only verified
   viewers generate qualified impressions, so audience quality outranks audience size.
   → See **[Authority Building](authority-building.md)** on network alignment.
4. **Originality is now an explicit, attributed signal.** Commentary is fine; commentary that
   is 90% someone else's screenshot is not. Add first-hand experience, original data, or a
   genuinely new frame.
5. **Bait packaging carries a durable cost.** "BREAKING" theatrics risk permanent payout
   deductions, on top of the slop/spam penalties the quality gate already applies.
6. **Volume still loses to quality.** Author diversity decay attenuates each successive post
   in a viewer's feed load, so burst posting to farm impressions works against itself.
   → See **[Content Strategy](content-strategy.md)**.
