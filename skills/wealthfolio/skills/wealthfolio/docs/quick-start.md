# Getting Started with Wealthfolio

Learn how to get started with Wealthfolio, the open-source desktop portfolio tracker.


Last updated November 12, 2026


Install Wealthfolio, add your accounts, and see your first dashboard – a few minutes, start to finish. For what each concept means, see <a href="/docs/concepts/" class="font-medium underline underline-offset-4">Core Concepts</a>.

## Installation

1.  Visit the <a href="/download/" class="font-medium underline underline-offset-4">download page</a> to get the latest version of Wealthfolio for your operating system.
2.  Run the installer and follow the on-screen instructions.
3.  Launch Wealthfolio after installation is complete.

## First Steps

### 1. Launch Wealthfolio

When launching Wealthfolio, you’ll be greeted with an onboarding wizard. This will guide you through the setup process and the key steps you’ll need to take to get started.


Your browser does not support HTML5 video. Here is a <a href="https://assets.wealthfolio.app/videos/onboarding.mp4" data-astro-cid-4itgdthg="">link to the video</a> instead.


### 2. Add Your Accounts

Add your investment, bank, or crypto accounts.

1.  Click **Add your accounts**, or go to **Settings → Accounts**.

2.  Fill out the account form:

    - **Account Name** – a display label (e.g. *Main Brokerage*).
    - **Account Group** – optional; groups related accounts on the dashboard (e.g. *RRSP*).
    - **Account Type** – Securities, Cash, Credit Card, or Crypto.
    - **Currency** – the account’s currency (set once, at creation).
    - **Tracking Mode** – **Transactions** (record every trade) or **Holdings** (enter balances as snapshots). See <a href="/docs/concepts/tracking-modes/" class="font-medium underline underline-offset-4">Tracking Modes</a>.

    See <a href="/docs/concepts/#account" class="font-medium underline underline-offset-4">Core Concepts</a> for what each field means.

3.  Click **Save**. Repeat for each account.

### 3. Add or import activities

Record your holdings and cash movements one of two ways:

1.  Open **Activities** in the sidebar.
2.  **Add manually**, or **upload a CSV**. See the <a href="/docs/guide/csv-import/" class="font-medium underline underline-offset-4">CSV Import guide</a> for broker-specific recipes.

The core app imports data manually or by CSV. For automatic broker sync, see the optional <a href="/connect/" class="font-medium underline underline-offset-4">Wealthfolio Connect</a>.

### 4. Explore Your Dashboards

After completing the initial setup, click “Let’s get started” to access your personalized Wealthfolio dashboard. Here, you can:

- View an overview of your financial situation, including:
  - Aggregated portfolio history
  - Total market value of your portfolio
  - Overall gain/loss percentage
  - Total gain/loss amount
- Set and monitor financial goals

- Account name
- Current market value
- Total gain/loss percentage
- Total gain/loss amount

This at-a-glance view allows you to quickly assess your overall financial health and the performance of individual accounts.
